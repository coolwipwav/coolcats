Competition Complete • 2026

One Cup. One Champion.

Five weeks of competition ended in extraordinary fashion as Rocky Mountain Villans overturned the standings with a record-breaking final performance.

🏆 2026 Champions Rocky Mountain Villans 74 Cup Points • Nolan

The Villans' 244.80 Fantrax point performance earned them first place in the final week and the tournament's five-point record bonus.

Without that bonus, Mauled By The Tigers would have won the competition. Instead, Nolan's side claimed the first trophy in Lonestar Premier League history.
