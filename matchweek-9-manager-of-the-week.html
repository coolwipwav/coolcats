From Eighth to Fourth: Diego's Leon Valley Are on the Rise

244.15 points, three victories, and just 0.43 points away from league history.

The Lonestar Times · Matchweek 9 · 2026

Just weeks ago, Leon Valley were sitting eighth in the Lonestar Premier League. Now, Diego has guided his club to fourth place, level with Wooburt City on 42 points.

His extraordinary 244.15 point performance earned him Matchweek 9 Manager of the Week honors, falling just 0.43 points short of Nolan's league record.

When questioned by WCCC Media, Diego appeared unfazed.

“Records are fun but they don’t give you trophies for those unfortunately.”

Meanwhile, MAULED BY THE TIGERS defender Jan Paul van Hecke claimed Player of the Week with 32.83 points, helping his club close the gap on league leaders Rocky Mountain Villans.

The former commissioner has his sights set on something bigger than records.
