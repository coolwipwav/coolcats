Matchweek 9 / Manager of the Week

FROM EIGHTH TO FOURTH: DIEGO'S LEON VALLEY STORM BACK INTO CONTENTION

The former commissioner claims Manager of the Week, narrowly misses a league record, while Van Hecke delivers the highest individual score of Matchweek 9.

By The Lonestar Times | Matchweek 9

Just weeks ago, Leon Valley were sitting eighth in the Lonestar Premier League standings. Questions surrounded their slow start, and a title challenge appeared increasingly unlikely.

Fast forward to Matchweek 9, and the conversation has changed entirely.

Diego has been named Manager of the Week after Leon Valley produced a remarkable 244.15 fantasy points, sweeping all three fixtures and climbing to fourth place.

The former league commissioner has now guided his club to a 14 win, 10 loss record, collecting 42 league points and drawing level with third placed Wooburt City Cool Cats.

Mohammed Kudus delivered 24 points, Jack Rudoni contributed 23.70, and Yasin Ayari added 23.42. Across the squad, Leon Valley delivered the kind of complete performance that has transformed their season.

JUST 0.43 POINTS FROM HISTORY

Diego's 244.15 point performance fell just 0.43 points short of the Lonestar Premier League's current single matchweek record, held by Nolan of Rocky Mountain Villans at 244.58 points.

When approached by WCCC Media, Diego appeared completely unaware of how close his squad had come to making history.

“Records are fun but they don’t give you trophies for those unfortunately.”

A fitting response from a manager whose priorities appear to extend well beyond individual accolades.

VAN HECKE STEALS THE INDIVIDUAL SPOTLIGHT

While Diego dominated the managerial headlines, Jan Paul van Hecke of MAULED BY THE TIGERS claimed Player of the Week with an extraordinary 32.83 fantasy points.

The defender scored a goal, recorded five shots, two on target, three key passes and a clean sheet in Tottenham's 2 to 0 victory over Crystal Palace.

His performance helped the Tigers secure three victories of their own, moving to 51 league points and closing the gap on Rocky Mountain Villans to just three.

THE TABLE TELLS THE STORY

Rocky Mountain Villans remain first with 54 points, followed closely by the Tigers on 51.

Wooburt City sit third with 42 points, but Leon Valley are now level with the Cool Cats and separated only by total fantasy points.

BUSTAiNBOLT, meanwhile, have fallen to fifth after suffering three defeats, opening the door for Diego's remarkable rise.

From eighth place to fourth, Leon Valley have gone from chasing the middle of the table to threatening the league's established contenders.

Diego stepped down as commissioner to focus on his club. Nine matchweeks into the season, that decision is beginning to look increasingly significant.

He gave up control of the league. Now he's coming for the top of it.
