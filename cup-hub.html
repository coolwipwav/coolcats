Five Star Shootout Cup

The first cup competition in Lonestar Premier League history is live.

Across Gameweeks 2–6, every club accumulates Shootout points based on its weekly finish. When the final scores are counted, the club with the highest total becomes the league's first-ever cup champion.

Five Star Shootout

The Race For The First Cup

Five gameweeks decide who gets their name written into Lonestar Premier League history.

The Decider

Everything Ends In Gameweek 6.

The Shootout has one final destination.

When Gameweek 6 ends, every Shootout point is added together. Somebody gets the trophy. Somebody gets the prize. And somebody gets to say they did it first.

The Silverware

There Can Only Be One First.

Years from now there may be league titles, cup winners, dynasties and trophy cabinets filled with silverware.

But there can only ever be one first trophy.

The winner of the Five Star Shootout becomes the first cup champion in Lonestar Premier League history.

Competition Format

Five Weeks Decide Everything.

Every weekly finish matters. First place earns 25 points, with points awarded down the table and a five-point bonus for the highest single-gameweek score of the competition.

After five weeks, every point is added together. Highest total takes the cup.

Winner's Prize

Silverware Isn't The Only Thing On The Line.

Thanks to Best Buy, the inaugural Five Star Shootout champion will also receive a $25 digital gift card.
