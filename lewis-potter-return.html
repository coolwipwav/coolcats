Transfers // The Return

Home
Again.

He left Wooburt City only days ago. Now Lewis-Potter is back — and one of the strangest pieces of transfer business in Cool Cats history has delivered far more than a homecoming.

WCCC Newsroom   //   September 19, 2026

Lewis-Potter wanted to come home.

Only days after an emotional departure from Wooburt City, the forward has completed a sensational return to the Cool Cats as part of a deal that has dramatically reshaped the squad.

His first spell had lasted four consecutive gameweeks. It was short by traditional football standards, but long enough for Lewis-Potter to establish himself as one of the recognizable faces of the young club.

The Original Goodbye

Wooburt City made the difficult decision to move Lewis-Potter in a deal for Alex Iwobi.

At the time, club management acknowledged the emotion surrounding the move while maintaining that football ultimately demanded difficult business decisions.

There was no bad blood. Lewis-Potter departed with the club's appreciation and best wishes.

Then came the twist: Lewis-Potter wanted to return.

The Door Reopens

What initially appeared to be the end of Lewis-Potter's chapter at Wooburt City quickly became something else entirely.

With an opportunity emerging to bring him back, Cool Cats management moved aggressively.

This time, however, Lewis-Potter would not be arriving alone.

Ryan Gravenberch was available as part of the wider transaction, presenting Wooburt City with an opportunity to reunite with Lewis-Potter while simultaneously adding another major piece to the squad.

Yoane Wissa departs Wooburt City as part of the deal that brings Lewis-Potter home.

Wissa Makes Way

To make the deal happen, Wooburt City parted ways with Yoane Wissa.

Wissa's departure represents the cost of an ambitious piece of business, but the return immediately transformed the complexion of the Cool Cats squad.

The Bigger Picture

Viewed as one continuous sequence of transactions, the scale of Wooburt City's business becomes clear.

Lewis-Potter's original departure delivered Alex Iwobi. His subsequent return came alongside Ryan Gravenberch at the cost of Wissa.

The net result is extraordinary: Wooburt City effectively turned Wissa into Lewis-Potter, Gravenberch and Iwobi.

Back Where He Belongs

Transfers are usually discussed in terms of value, squad building and long-term planning.

This one carries something else.

Lewis-Potter left Wooburt City with the door seemingly closed. Days later, that door reopened.

The Cool Cats got their player back. Lewis-Potter got his homecoming. And Wooburt City emerged with a squad stronger than the one it had before the entire saga began.

Welcome Home, LP.

Lewis-Potter has officially rejoined Wooburt City Cool Cats FC.
