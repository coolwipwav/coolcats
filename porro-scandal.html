WCCC Exclusive // League Investigation

Leon Valley
Breaks Silence

Allegations surrounding Pedro Porro, questions over the future of Leon Valley's captain, and an extraordinary war of words have become the league's latest controversy.

WCCC Newsroom   //   September 20, 2026

Tensions between Wooburt City Cool Cats and Leon Valley Football Club have escalated following allegations surrounding Cool Cats defender Pedro Porro.

League sources have raised allegations that the owner and manager of Leon Valley Football Club has developed an unusually close relationship with Porro, with speculation suggesting an attempt may be underway to entice the player away from Wooburt City.

At this stage, there is no confirmed evidence of an agreement between Porro and Leon Valley, and no transfer involving the player has been announced.

Questions at Leon Valley

The controversy comes amid separate questions surrounding the future of Leon Valley captain Jarrad.

Jarrad's appearance on the trade block had generated speculation about whether the club could be prepared to move its captain. Increased attention surrounding the situation only added to questions about Leon Valley's plans.

With rumors spreading throughout the league, WCCC Newsroom approached Leon Valley directly and requested a response regarding both the captain's situation and the allegations involving Porro.

Leon Valley captain Jarrad has found himself at the center of speculation surrounding the club's trade plans.

Leon Valley Responds

Leon Valley strongly rejected the narrative surrounding the club and offered an emphatic defense of its captain.

Statement // Leon Valley Football Club

“We do not respond to rumors. There is clear evidence that we have stood our ground on any negotiations involving Jarrad as he is our skipper who was voted for by his teammates who highly regard him.”

“The trade block isn't anything but a tool. It doesn't fully represent our plans and vision.”

“We at our football club are beyond delighted to have someone like Jarrad leading us on and off the field. He grew up in Leon Valley for Pete's sake. We couldn't have asked for a better captain. He lives and breathes Leon Valley.”

The club then addressed the Porro allegations directly — and turned its attention toward Wooburt City.

Statement Continued

“To answer this childish rumor about an affair, there is nothing involving Wooburt City and Leon Valley regarding any sorts of trades at the moment.”

“We believe there is someone amongst their brass spreading all these Pedro Porro rumors, and for that, we will not be dealing with Wooburt City until they deal with the rumors internally.”

A Relationship Fractured

Leon Valley's response represents a significant escalation. What began as speculation surrounding one player has now developed into a public dispute between two clubs.

The assertion that the rumors originated from within Wooburt City places further pressure on Cool Cats leadership and raises a new question: who, if anyone, inside the club has been discussing Porro's relationship with Leon Valley?

For now, Porro remains a Wooburt City player, Jarrad remains Leon Valley's captain, and no transaction between the two clubs has been announced.

But after Leon Valley's declaration that it will refuse to conduct business with Wooburt City until the matter is addressed internally, relations between the clubs appear to have reached their lowest point yet.

Developing Story

WCCC Newsroom will update this report as further statements, transactions or developments emerge.
