Inside Wooburt City // After Matchday 5 A difficult week spills into the dressing room.

A difficult Gameweek 5 has already produced its first casualty.

Wooburt City Cool Cats have officially parted ways with defender Levi Colwill, bringing an abrupt end to his time at the club following what sources describe as a tense post-match dressing-room exchange.

The Cool Cats stumbled to a 1–2 record in Gameweek 5, and frustrations reportedly boiled over behind closed doors afterward. Sources close to the club say words were exchanged inside the dressing room that left management convinced a change needed to be made.

Exactly what was said will remain inside the room. What happened next will not.

Colwill Has Been Cut.

Club officials moved quickly, viewing the situation as something that needed to be dealt with before it could develop into a larger distraction.
