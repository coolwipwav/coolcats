The Five Star Shootout Cup // The first cup competition in Lonestar Premier League history
The Lonestar Times Five Star Shootout Cup Competition Gameweeks 2–6

The Lonestar Premier League is proud to officially introduce the Five Star Shootout Cup — the first cup competition in league history.

And with the competition concluding in Gameweek 6, somebody is about to become the league's first-ever cup champion.

A New Piece Of League History

The Five Star Shootout was created with one simple idea: reward the team that can consistently put up points when it matters.

Across Gameweeks 2–6, every club accumulates Shootout points based on its weekly finish. First place earns 25 points, with points awarded down the table — plus a five-point bonus for producing the highest single-gameweek score during the competition.
