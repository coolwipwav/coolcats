A New Era For The Lonestar League: New Look, New Home — And The First Trophy Is Almost Here

The H-E-B Lonestar League unveils its new identity and official website as the Five Star Shootout prepares for a dramatic Gameweek 6 finale.

Published By The Lonestar Times Competition H-E-B Lonestar Dynasty Premier League

The Lonestar League has a new look.

And now, it has a new home.

League officials have officially unveiled the new H-E-B Lonestar League identity, marking the beginning of a more ambitious era for the competition. Alongside the refreshed league branding comes the launch of the league’s new official website, designed to become the central home for tables, club information, league news, controversies and everything else surrounding the competition.

The change represents more than a fresh coat of paint.

What began as a fantasy competition between friends is increasingly developing its own history, rivalries and culture — and the league now has an identity built to match it.

From Wooburt City and Leon Valley to Children of Emenike, Space Cadets and the rest of the league, every club now competes beneath one banner:

The H-E-B
Lonestar League.

One league // Ten clubs

And the timing couldn't be better.

Because the first piece of silverware in league history is about to be awarded.

The Five Star Shootout Reaches Its Final Week

While the new website and league identity mark the future, there is still unfinished business on the pitch.

The inaugural Five Star Shootout, sponsored by Best Buy, reaches its conclusion next week in Gameweek 6.

The five-week competition has transformed every point scored into part of a larger race for the league's first trophy.

Each week, clubs are ranked by their total fantasy score. First place earns 25 Shootout points, with the remaining positions receiving 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4 and 2 points.

There is also one potentially decisive wrinkle:

The highest individual single-week fantasy score across the entire competition earns a five-point bonus.

After Gameweek 6, the points from all five rounds will be added together.

The club sitting at the top wins the Five Star Shootout Cup.

One trophy.

One winner.

And with the finish line now in sight, there is nowhere left to hide.

More Than A Trophy

The winner won't leave with bragging rights alone.

Thanks to Best Buy's sponsorship of the Five Star Shootout, the inaugural champion will also receive a $25 Best Buy digital gift card.

But years from now, the prize money probably won't be what anyone remembers.

Someone is about to become the first trophy winner in H-E-B Lonestar League history.

Every champion that follows will have somebody to compare themselves against.

Every club that falls short will know exactly who got there first.

And after four weeks of positioning, Gameweek 6 is the final chance to change the story.
