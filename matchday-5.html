It was not the weekend Wooburt City Cool Cats had envisioned.

The Cool Cats leave Matchday 5 with a 1–2 record, scoring 184.29 points across three fixtures. It was enough to take one victory, but two defeats see WCCC slip to second in the table at 8–0–4, level on 24 points with new leaders Mauled By A Tiger.

There were still plenty of reasons for optimism.

Kevin Schade was magnificent, producing a team-leading 21.79 points after registering two assists. Captain Alexander Isak delivered again with 16.76, while Alex Iwobi (14.71), Andy Robertson (14.44) and returning fan favorite Keane Lewis-Potter (13.70) all made important contributions.

But naturally, nobody around Wooburt City is talking about any of that.

The Story Everyone Is Talking About They’re talking about Pedro Porro.

18 Minutes.
That’s Interesting.

Just days after WCCC Media reported allegations surrounding Leon Valley’s interest in Pedro Porro, the defender took the field for Tottenham.

He lasted 18 minutes.
