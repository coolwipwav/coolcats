A New Era: The Lonestar Premier League Finds Its Identity

A league born between friends is beginning to build an identity of its own.

The Lonestar Times September 2026 League HQ
The new identity of the Lonestar Premier League // Est. 2026
The Lonestar Times League Identity Est. 2026

The Lonestar Premier League started simply; a group of friends, a love of football, and the belief that competing against each other should probably mean far more than it reasonably should.

Then something happened.

Rivalries formed. Trades became headlines. Players became heroes and villains. Clubs started developing personalities and histories of their own.

What began as a fantasy competition started becoming its own little football world.

Now, that world finally has a look to call its own.

A New Identity

The Lonestar Premier League is proud to unveil its new visual identity, official website and partnership with H-E-B.

The new league logos give the competition something it previously never truly had: a badge of its own.

The identity embraces our Texas roots while representing what we've become — an American league whose clubs aren't confined by state lines.

The Website A Home For Our History

A Home For Our History

The new Lonestar Premier League website gives the league a permanent home.

Standings tell us who won. The website will remember everything else — the trades, rivalries, controversies, cup runs, ridiculous rumors and unforgettable performances that make this league ours.

Over time, those stories become history.

And now we'll have somewhere to keep it.

Club Identity Build The Lore

Managers: Build Your Club

With the league establishing its identity, we're encouraging every manager to give their own club one too.

Make a crest. Pick your colors. Create something that's yours.

It doesn't need to be professional or complicated. Reference your city, neighborhood, personality, an inside joke — whatever represents your club.

Because eventually that crest becomes attached to moments.

A huge upset. A blockbuster trade. A cup victory. A bitter rivalry.

That's how club identity develops, and collectively, those identities help build the lore of the Lonestar Premier League.
