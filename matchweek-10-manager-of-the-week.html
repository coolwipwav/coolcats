THE NOLAN STANDARD: VILLANS STRENGTHEN THEIR GRIP ON FIRST PLACE

209.17 fantasy points. Three victories. A 21–6 record. Rocky Mountain Villans continue to set the pace.

Manager of the Week
Rocky Mountain Villans
209.17Matchweek fantasy points
21–6League record · First place

Three fixtures. Three victories. One team setting the standard.

Ten matchweeks into the inaugural Lonestar Premier League season, one manager continues to set the standard.

Nolan.

The Rocky Mountain Villans manager delivered 209.17 fantasy points in Matchweek 10, sweeping all three league fixtures and improving his club's record to 21 wins and 6 losses.

The performance earned Nolan another Manager of the Week honor and reinforced the Villans' position at the top of the league table.

An Attack Firing on All Cylinders

Noni Madueke led the way with 26.54 fantasy points, recording four key passes, four shots and two efforts on target.

Nicolas Jackson followed with 22.78 points, contributing a goal and an assist, while Youri Tielemans delivered 20.99 with a goal and four key passes.

Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall added 20.65 points, scoring once to complete a quartet of attacking standouts.

Together, those four players produced 90.96 fantasy points, nearly half of Nolan's total.

The Difference Between the Posts

The Villans' attacking performances may have commanded attention, but their goalkeepers were equally important.

Robin Roefs and James Trafford combined for 34.27 points, each recording a clean sheet and four saves.

It was a remarkable contribution from a position that can often determine the difference between a good matchweek and an exceptional one.

And the performance was not without setbacks. Harry Maguire managed just 0.59 points, while Morgan Rogers finished with 8.32.

Yet even with those disappointing returns, the Villans comfortably surpassed the 200 point mark.

First Place Is No Accident

With three more victories secured, Rocky Mountain Villans now sit atop the Lonestar Premier League with a 21–6 record.

Nolan has already guided his club to the inaugural Five Star Shootout Cup title. Now, his attention remains firmly on the league championship.

The Villans are building a campaign defined by consistency, depth and an ability to deliver when the pressure rises.

His latest Manager of the Week performance is another reminder that the Villans are not simply benefiting from an impressive opening stretch.

As the competition approaches its next chapter, the rest of the league faces an increasingly uncomfortable reality.

To win the Lonestar Premier League, somebody is going to have to take it from Nolan.
