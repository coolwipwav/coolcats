🏆 Five Star Shootout Cup • 2026 Champions

Rocky Mountain Villans Are Crowned Champions.

An extraordinary final week performance sees Rocky Mountain Villans snatch the Lonestar Premier League's inaugural cup from Mauled By The Tigers in one of the most dramatic finishes imaginable.

The Lonestar Times • Cup Final Report • 2026
Rocky Mountain Villans receive the inaugural Five Star Shootout Cup.

The first trophy in Lonestar Premier League history has found its home.

And it is heading to the Rocky Mountains.

After five weeks of competition, shifting standings, and a championship battle that came down to the smallest of margins, Rocky Mountain Villans have officially been crowned champions of the Five Star Shootout Cup.

But this was no ordinary cup victory.

Nolan's Villans entered the final week trailing the competition's leading clubs, with Mauled By The Tigers seemingly positioned to claim the league's inaugural silverware.

What followed was nothing short of extraordinary.

A stunning 244.80 Fantrax point performance, the highest single week score recorded throughout the entire tournament, propelled Rocky Mountain Villans to the top of the standings.

And when the final calculations were completed, the championship belonged to Nolan.

Villans 74 • Tigers 71 Three points separated glory from heartbreak.

A Final Week For The History Books

Entering the final week, Mauled By The Tigers held a commanding position with 61 cup points.

Wooburt City Cool Cats followed closely behind on 57, while Rocky Mountain Villans sat further back with 44.

For Nolan, the situation was clear.

A respectable performance would not be enough.

The Villans needed something special.

And special is exactly what they delivered.

Rocky Mountain Villans produced a remarkable 244.80 Fantrax points, finishing first in the final week's scoring and securing the maximum 25 cup points available.

Their nearest challenger on the week, Tzolid, managed 182.53.

The difference was staggering.

More importantly, Rocky Mountain Villans had just produced the single highest score of the entire Five Star Shootout Cup.

And that achievement would change everything.

The Five Point Bonus That Decided A Championship

One of the defining features of the Five Star Shootout Cup was its tournament scoring bonus.

An additional five cup points would be awarded to the club responsible for the highest individual gameweek score across the entire competition.

For much of the tournament, that distinction belonged to Ravensgate FC, whose impressive 238.25 point performance had established the benchmark.

Until Nolan arrived.

With 244.80 points in the final week, Rocky Mountain Villans surpassed Ravensgate's record and claimed the tournament's five point bonus.

Those five points proved decisive.

Without the bonus, Rocky Mountain Villans would have finished the tournament on 69 points.

Mauled By The Tigers would have been champions with 71.

Instead, the bonus lifted Nolan's side to 74.

One extraordinary performance had completely reversed the championship outcome.

A competition that appeared destined for one club had been claimed by another in its final moments.

Heartbreak For Mauled By The Tigers

2026 Cup Runners Up Mauled By The Tigers 71 cup points. A remarkable campaign that ended just three points short of the inaugural title.

For Mauled By The Tigers, the conclusion will be particularly difficult to accept.

Having entered the final week with 61 cup points, Mauled appeared to have one hand on the trophy.

Their final week performance of 167.49 Fantrax points earned another 10 cup points, bringing their tournament total to 71.

In almost any other scenario, that would have been enough.

But the Villans had other ideas.

Mauled finished just three points behind the eventual champions, becoming the inaugural competition's runners up despite leading the race heading into the final week.

The result serves as a reminder of the unforgiving nature of cup football.

Sometimes, even an excellent tournament is not enough.

Wooburt City Claim Third

2026 Cup Third Place Wooburt City Cool Cats 65 cup points. The Cool Cats secure a place on the inaugural Five Star Shootout Cup podium.

Wooburt City Cool Cats also entered the final week with genuine hopes of lifting the trophy.

Sitting second on 57 cup points, WCCC remained firmly in contention.

However, a final week score of 164.64 Fantrax points was only enough for sixth place on the week, earning eight additional cup points.

The Cool Cats finished the tournament with 65 points, securing third place overall.

For a club with ambitions of becoming one of the league's dominant forces, the result represents another podium finish, but also another reminder of the fine margins separating contenders from champions.

With the inaugural cup now decided, attention will inevitably turn toward the league campaign and the remaining opportunities for silverware.

A New Name In Lonestar History

When the Five Star Shootout Cup was first announced, it represented something more than another competition.

It was an opportunity for the Lonestar Premier League to establish its first piece of silverware, create new rivalries, and begin building a history that future clubs would attempt to match.

Now, that history has its first champion.

Rocky Mountain Villans did not simply win the competition.

They produced its defining moment.

A final week performance that broke the tournament scoring record, overturned the championship standings, and denied Mauled By The Tigers what had appeared to be a deserved triumph.

For Nolan, it is a remarkable achievement.

For the rest of the league, it is a warning.

The Rocky Mountain Villans have announced themselves as a serious force in the Lonestar Premier League.

And as the inaugural champions celebrate, one fact will remain permanently etched into the competition's history.

The first Five Star Shootout Cup belongs to Rocky Mountain Villans.
